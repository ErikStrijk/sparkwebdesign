# Surface: Sparkdesign one-pager (src/pages/index.astro)

Mode: Persuade. Mobile-first. Action: "Vraag je gratis ontwerp aan" (form at #contact), WhatsApp secondary. Reassurance beside every CTA: "€0 vooraf · je betaalt pas als je site live staat". Proof = numbers (€500, 21 dagen, 3 rondes, €0 vooraf); reviews/portfolio/photo are visible placeholders. User chose the category standard; quality bar = Stripe.com finish level.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard for a small studio, executed at Stripe-level precision: numbers instead of adjectives, one action repeated everywhere. Refuses the loose template look: gray image boxes, pill badges, icon-card grids.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper (#F9F0E4) and sand (#F3E5D1) ground. Coffee ink (#221915) carries headings, numbers, the header pill and the dark bands. Coral (#FF5A36) with ink text is used only for actions and one highlight. Bricolage Grotesque for display, Figtree for text. Hairline rules, offset soft shadows, 12px radius.

STORY: The visitor understands price, time and risk in one viewport, believes it from the concrete numbers and the steps, and requests a free design.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed warm workspace photo (Clay Banks, Unsplash) behind the hero. Desktop: paper-colored fade on the left behind dark copy, photo clear on the right with the white form card on top. Mobile: photo strip at top fading into paper, then copy and form.

FORM: Category standard (canon), chosen over the assigned roll; seed:2dce2759cf5c22a7

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the numbers bar counts up once on entry (reduced motion: static). A sticky mobile CTA bar appears after the hero.

Open: what hosting €10/maand includes; extra-cost list; who writes the copy; response time; WhatsApp number; Web3Forms key.
